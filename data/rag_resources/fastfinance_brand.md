# Fast Finance — Brand & Compliance Guidelines

Tone:
- Clear, professional, and supportive.
- Confident but not promotional or exaggerated.

Forbidden Claims:
- No "guaranteed approval"
- No "instant approval"
- No "immediate access"
- No timelines such as "24 hours", "same day", or similar.

Language Rules:
- Use conditional phrasing: "may", "could", "subject to".
- Avoid absolutes.

Mandatory Safety Line:
- Always include: "T&Cs and eligibility apply."
